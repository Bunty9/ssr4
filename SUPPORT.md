# Getting help

- **Read the guides first**: [docs/](docs/) covers fabrication, firmware, bring-up and
  installation step by step.
- **Questions and ideas**: open a discussion or an issue. Say which board revision (commit) you
  have and what you already tried.
- **Something is wrong in the design or firmware**: use the "Bug report" issue template.
- **Safety or security problem**: report it privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

This is a hobby project maintained in spare time. There is no commercial support and no
warranty: the hardware and firmware are provided "as is" under their licences, and the board is
**not certified** (see [docs/safety.md](docs/safety.md)).
